# Key Findings

1. Accuracy improved from 72.0% at N=1 to 78.8% at N=16.
2. The largest marginal improvement occurred from N=4 to N=8: +3.6
   percentage points.
3. N=8 achieved 78.4%, only 0.4 percentage points below N=16.
4. N=16 used approximately 16.0 times the N=1 inference compute.
5. N=8 therefore represents a substantially better observed accuracy–compute
   operating point than N=16 in this experiment.
6. Additional sampling recovered 42 questions that were wrong at N=1 but
   correct at N=16.
7. Ninety questions remained stable-wrong across all evaluated budgets.
8. Twenty-one questions were regressed or unstable across budgets.

These are empirical observations for the frozen model, benchmark, and setup;
they do not establish broader causality.
